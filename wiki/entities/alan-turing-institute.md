# Alan Turing Institute

## Overview

UK's national institute for data science and AI, founded in 2015. Focuses on research, policy, and responsible AI deployment.

## Key Contributions

- AI Ethics and Governance in Practice Programme
- Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard
- AI Explainability in Practice workbook (2024)

## Impact

Primary UK institution bridging academic research and public policy for responsible AI adoption.
