# European Union

## Overview

Political and economic union of 27 European countries that established the world's first comprehensive AI regulation with the EU AI Act (2023).

## Key Contributions

- Landmark AI Act legislation establishing risk-based regulatory framework
- Global standard-setting in AI governance alongside GDPR model
