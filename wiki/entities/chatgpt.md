# ChatGPT

## Overview

Conversational AI assistant developed by OpenAI, built on GPT architecture. Launched November 2022, gained widespread adoption for educational, creative, and productivity tasks.

## Key Contributions

- Made LLM capabilities accessible to general public
- Sparked debate on AI's role in education and knowledge work
- Subject of "Cognitive Debt" research (MIT Media Lab, 2025)

## Impact

Estimated 100M+ users within first 2 months. Study findings suggest measurable cognitive offloading effects when used for writing tasks, with reduced neural connectivity and ownership perception among users.
