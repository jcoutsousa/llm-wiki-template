# Andrew Ng

## Overview

AI researcher and entrepreneur, co-founder of Coursera, former head of Google Brain and Baidu AI Group. Created Machine Learning Yearning (2018) and founded deeplearning.ai.

## Key Contributions

- Popularized deep learning through online courses
- Authored "Machine Learning Yearning" strategic guide
- Founded deeplearning.ai education platform

## Impact

One of the most influential educators in AI; his courses have reached millions of learners globally.
