# AI Explainability in Practice

## Executive Summary

The Alan Turing Institute's workbook on operationalizing AI explainability for public sector practitioners. Provides practical tools for implementing transparent and accountable AI systems through six maxims: Be Transparent, Be Accountable, Consider Context, Reflect on Impacts.

## Key Findings

- **Four Maxims of Explainability**:
  1. Be Transparent - disclose AI use, meaningfully explain decisions
  2. Be Accountable - assign responsibility, justify/evidence decisions, show explanations provided
  3. Consider Context - choose appropriate models, tailor governance, identify audience
  4. Reflect on Impacts - ensure individual/societal wellbeing

- **Six Explanation Types**: Rationale, Responsibility, Data, Fairness, Safety, Impact explanations
- **Process vs Outcome-Based**: Two complementary approaches to explanation
- **Child-Centered Considerations**: Specific guidance for systems affecting children, referencing UNICEF policy guidance

## Methodology

- Co-designed with UK citizens and public sector bodies
- Part of AI Ethics and Governance in Practice Programme
- Includes hands-on workshop activities and templates

## Impact

Reference for UK public sector AI governance; integrates GDPR accountability principles with technical XAI methods.
