# EU AI Act: Council and Parliament Deal on AI Regulation

## Executive Summary

Landmark EU legislation establishing the world's first comprehensive AI regulation based on a risk-based approach. Covers rules for high-impact AI models, governance mechanisms, and prohibitions on unacceptable AI practices.

## Key Findings

- **Risk-Based Approach**: AI systems classified by potential harm - higher risk triggers stricter rules
- **High-Risk AI**: Extensive requirements for data quality, technical documentation, human oversight
- **Prohibited Practices**: 
  - Cognitive behavioural manipulation
  - Untargeted scraping of facial images
  - Emotion recognition in workplace/education
  - Social scoring
  - Biometric categorization for sensitive inference
- **Governance**: Revised EU-level enforcement with oversight bodies
- **Transparency**: Light transparency obligations for limited-risk AI (disclosure of AI-generated content)

## Impact

Sets global standard for AI regulation, similar to GDPR's influence. Balances innovation promotion with fundamental rights protection.
