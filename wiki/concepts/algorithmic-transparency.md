# Algorithmic Transparency

## Definition

The principle that AI systems should be open to inspection and free from secrets, enabling stakeholders to understand how decisions are made.

## Related Work

- UK Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS) - co-developed by Central Digital and Data Office with The Alan Turing Institute
- EU AI Act - requires transparency obligations for AI-generated content disclosure

## Applications

- Public sector AI deployments
- Regulatory compliance
- Stakeholder trust building
