# Explainable AI

## Definition

XAI (Explainable AI) refers to processes and methods that help humans understand and trust AI model outputs. Focuses on making black-box model decisions interpretable through supplementary techniques and tools.

## History

- 2010s: Growing concern about deep learning opacity
- 2018: EU GDPR introduced "right to explanation"
- 2020s: Development of technical XAI methods (LIME, SHAP, attention visualization)
- 2024: Alan Turing Institute formalized explainability maxims for public sector

## Applications

- Healthcare: Clinical decision support explanations
- Finance: Credit scoring justification
- Public sector: Social care, policing, education
- Regulatory compliance: GDPR, EU AI Act transparency requirements
