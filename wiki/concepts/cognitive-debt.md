# Cognitive Debt

## Definition

The accumulation of reduced cognitive engagement and knowledge retention when relying heavily on external tools like LLMs or search engines for cognitive tasks. Term popularized by MIT Media Lab research (2025).

## Key Findings

- LLM-assisted essay writing showed reduced neural connectivity (alpha, beta, theta, delta bands)
- Users demonstrated weaker memory recall and quoting ability
- Brain-to-LLM transition (writing first, then using LLM) showed more neural re-engagement than LLM-to-Brain transition
