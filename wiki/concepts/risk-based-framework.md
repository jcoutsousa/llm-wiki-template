# Risk-Based Framework

## Definition

Regulatory approach classifying AI systems by potential harm, applying stricter requirements to higher-risk applications. Used in EU AI Act (2023).

## Categories

1. **Prohibited** - Unacceptable risk (social scoring, emotion recognition in workplace)
2. **High-Risk** - Strict requirements for safety, transparency, human oversight
3. **Limited Risk** - Light transparency obligations (disclosure of AI content)
4. **Minimal Risk** - No specific requirements
