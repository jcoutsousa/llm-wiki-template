# AI Regulation

## Definition

Rules and governance frameworks for AI development and deployment to ensure safety, fairness, and alignment with societal values.

## Key Frameworks

- **EU AI Act** (2023) - Risk-based approach with prohibitions and high-risk requirements
- **Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard** (UK) - Framework for public sector disclosure
